Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my order?

Log in and check My Orders for real-time status — Pending, Processing, Shipped, or Delivered. You'll also see the order ID to reference if you contact us.

How do I return an item?

Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. See our full Shipping & Returns Policy for eligibility and steps.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery, UPI, and major debit/credit cards.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are typically delivered within 3–7 business days after dispatch, depending on your location.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Email us as soon as possible with your order ID — we can usually cancel or adjust an order before it ships.
