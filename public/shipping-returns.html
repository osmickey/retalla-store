Shipping & Returns Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Shipping

We ship across India. Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days of confirmation.

  • Free shipping on all orders over Rs. 499.
  • A flat shipping charge of Rs. 49 applies to orders below Rs. 499.
  • Estimated delivery: 3–7 business days depending on your location, after dispatch.
  • You'll receive an order status update as your package moves from Processing → Shipped → Delivered — track it anytime under My Orders.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on eligible orders. Please have the exact order amount ready for our delivery partner at the time of delivery.

Returns

We want you to be happy with your purchase. Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they are:

  • Unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags intact.
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (your order confirmation).

Certain items — such as innerwear/lingerie and personal care products — cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, unless received damaged or defective.

How to Request a Return

Contact us at support@retalla.in with your order number and reason for return within 7 days of delivery. We'll confirm the pickup or drop-off process for your item.

Refunds

Once your returned item is received and inspected, we'll process your refund to the original payment method (or as store credit for COD orders) within 5–7 business days.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item — we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund.

Contact Us

Questions about your order, shipping, or a return? Email us at support@retalla.in.
